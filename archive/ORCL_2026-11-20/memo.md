# IC Memo: ORCL (Oracle Corporation)
**Date:** 2026-11-20 | **Price:** $102.45 | **Market cap:** $318,614M | **Verdict:** WATCH
**Conviction:** Low | **Position:** None

## Verdict
WATCH. At $102.45 the stock is 36% below the $159.39 base value, but the base value depends on a sales-to-capital of 0.5-1.5. Observed capital turnover is about 0.13, and that dispute is the biggest unresolved risk. This becomes a BUY if the Q2 FY27 report on 2026-12-14 shows revenue growth of at least 30% and RPO of at least $664B, with no new raise beyond the $20B cap.

## Thesis
- The backlog is large and verified: RPO is $664B, with 13% due within 12 months (V5). That is 9.2x TTM revenue, and the base case needs only $194B of FY30 revenue against the $225B+ guide.
- The price already assumes a slowdown: $102.45 implies a 10-year revenue CAGR of 11.5% or a 26.4% margin. Today revenue is growing 30% and the adjusted margin is 35.0% (equity reverse DCF).
- The financing for now is in place: the $10B stock sale and $10B of notes closed 2026-11-04 (V2, V4), and pro forma cash is $56,905M.

## Macro backdrop
Headwind, Medium. The 10Y is at 5.07%, markets price a 91-93% chance of a Dec 9 hike, and new Oracle debt costs 7.00-8.65% (V4). This weighs on a company with $125.3B of borrowings and on cash flows that sit mostly in the terminal value (78.6% of the base).

## Valuation
Bear / base / bull: $47.09 / $159.39 / $314.39 (bottom-up beta 1.56; industry beta gives $151.64 for the base). The price sits in the lower third of the range, above the short seller's $67. The terminal-growth test runs from $137.19 to $178.90, and with ROC = WACC the value is $129.27. Every one of those figures is above the price, but each still assumes capital turns over far faster than it does today.
