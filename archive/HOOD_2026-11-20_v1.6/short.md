# Short seller: HOOD, 2026-11-20
**Bear case price:** $31.42 (-51.5% vs $64.77) | **Thesis strength:** 2/5

## 1. Top 3 bear points
1. **All of the growth is event contracts, and that line is under legal and tax attack.** Event contracts were $146M of the $150M Q3 revenue increase. Revenue outside event contracts grew 0.3% y/y (10-Q, 2026-10-28, EDGAR). Several risk factors are new since the Q2 10-Q. The Ninth Circuit denied relief to RHD against Nevada. Robinhood has "stop[ped] offering certain new event contracts" in some states. The CFTC proposed (Oct 9) defining event contracts as swaps. North Carolina (6%) and Kentucky (14.25%) taxes on prediction-market operators start 2027-01-01, and the filing also names a federal wagering excise tax risk (EDGAR). Meta is building a rival app (web, no 8-K).
2. **Earnings are getting worse under a revenue beat.** In Q3, pretax income fell 1.4% while revenue rose 11.8%. Net income fell 15.8% as the tax rate rose from 12.3% to 23.8%. The 9-month result includes a one-time $106M gain from deconsolidating RVI and a California valuation-allowance release. Q3 then took a $60M mark-to-market loss on RVI (10-Q, EDGAR). Credit provisions rose 173% y/y. Card write-offs were $31M vs $5M.
3. **Credit risk and customer leverage are rising, and rate hikes no longer help.** Purchased card receivables rose 92% in nine months ($786M to $1.51B), and allowances are now 8.1% of on-balance-sheet cards. Receivables from users rose 31% to $23.6B. Intraday margin went to 4x in October, and 10x crypto perpetuals are planned (10-Q, EDGAR). Net interest fell 9% despite the September hike. The CEO sold $148.9M at an average $119.15 and set up a new plan to sell 1.9M more shares (Form 4s and 10-Q Item 5, EDGAR).

## 2. Weakest assumption
**Base-case growth of 15% (ROE 22%).** It should be about 8%, the analyst's own bear driver. Outside event contracts revenue is flat, crypto fell 55%, and the event-contract line now faces state bans and new taxes. On 8% growth the analyst's own model gives $11.87. The bull price also leans on the 0.60 industry beta ($40.31). For a business with 12% event-contract and 8% crypto revenue, that beta is not credible.

## 4. Where the macro call is wrong
The macro report says "a hike lifts income." Q3 shows the opposite: net interest fell $41M (-9%) after the September hike. Cash Sweep yields only 0.59%, the High-Yield Cash program pays customers more, and securities lending weakened. The rate-sensitivity table leaves out Cash Sweep (10-Q, EDGAR). With 5.7% three-month inflation and card delinquencies rising from 4.7% to 5.5%, this looks like a Headwind, not Neutral.
