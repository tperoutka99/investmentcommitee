# IC Memo: SPCX (Space Exploration Technologies Corp.)
**Date:** 2026-11-19 | **Price:** $138.43 | **Market cap:** $1,880.0B | **Verdict:** PASS
**Conviction:** High | **Position:** None

## Verdict
PASS. At $138.43 the stock trades above the analyst's own bull value ($110.21). To justify the price, revenue would have to grow about 39% a year for 10 years. Half of Q3 revenue came from two customers, and the AI revenue sits on contracts either side can end on 90 days' notice.

## Thesis
- The price assumes about $1.59T of revenue in year 10, against a $58B run-rate today, or a 70.5% operating margin against 19.1% now.
- The quality of earnings is thin. Free cash flow over nine months was −$35.0B. Adjusted EBITDA of $8.8B leaves out $2.36B of stock compensation (16% of revenue). Starlink made 85% of operating income.
- Customer B was 34.5% of Q3 revenue (Verified). Against that, SpaceX has $37.8B of commitments it cannot cancel, $25.6B of them due in 2027.

## Macro backdrop
**Headwind, medium confidence.** The 10-year yield is 5.07% and futures price a December hike at more than 90%. That hurts a stock whose base value is 86% terminal value. The dollar is up 6.3% in 3 months, and Starlink revenue per consumer fell from $83 to $69, which points to a more foreign customer mix.

## Valuation
The analyst's DCF gives bear **$11.46**, base **$49.95** and bull **$110.21**. The short seller's sum of the parts gives about $26 (range $20–35). At $138.43 the price is above the whole range, and it is 199x Starlink's annualized operating income. Correcting net cash adds $38.6B, under $3/share on 14,112M diluted shares (my arithmetic), while adding the $40B lease pro forma takes off $2.83/share. Since Dec-25, each $1 of new capital has added $0.44 of revenue, against 0.9 in the model, so even the base case is generous.

## Data flags
- **Market cap Verified at $1,880.0B** (13,581.1M shares). FMP's figure is understated. No effect on the view.
- **Cash classification Contradicted:** net cash is about $51.0B, not $12.45B. A small positive that does not matter against a $1.88T market cap.
- **Customer concentration Verified.** This strengthens bear point #1.
- **Anthropic contract Verified, but Customer B's identity is not confirmed** and the mid-2027 exit report was Not found. I ignore the rumor. The risk stands on the 10-Q terms alone.
- **Lockups Verified:** 328.4M–797.6M shares on Dec 8, then up to 351.9M more after Q2'27 results. This is supply pressure on the price.
- **Tesla combination Verified.** It is an undetermined event, and it adds governance risk.
- **Q4 capex of $35–40B Not found.** I disregard it. Verified Q3 capex of $19,593M is already above Q2's $18.4B.

## Kill criteria
- The price closes at or below $50 (base $49.95). Then re-underwrite as a BUY candidate.
- A 10-Q or 8-K shows SI contracts amended to remove the 90-day termination right, or Customer B falling below 20% of quarterly revenue while total revenue grows.
- A quarter with positive free cash flow (operating cash flow minus capex) and capex at or below $19.6B.
