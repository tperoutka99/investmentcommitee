# IC Memo: ADBE (Adobe Inc.)
**Date:** 2026-11-20 | **Price:** $225.38 | **Market cap:** $87.7B | **Verdict:** BUY
**Conviction:** Medium | **Position:** Starter

## Verdict
We buy a starter position. At $225.38 the stock trades at the analyst's bear case ($223). The price implies revenue falling 1.6% a year for 10 years, while verified ARR is still growing 11.2%. The organic slowdown is real and Q4 (Dec 9) is a real test, so we size small and add only if Q4 ARR holds.

## Thesis
- **The price assumes decline.** The reverse DCF needs revenue to fall 1.6% a year, or the margin to drop to 20%. Q3 ARR was $27.50B, up 11.2% (verified).
- **Cheap cash machine.** EV/EBIT is 9.4x against 20–42x for peers. FCF yield is 12.3% (9.9% after stock pay), and TTM FCF of $10.59B exceeds net income of $7.28B.
- **Balance sheet and buybacks.** Strict cash is $5,639M (verified) against net debt of $724M. Diluted shares fell 6.3% TTM, with $24.55B of buyback authority left.

## Macro backdrop
**Headwind, Medium.** An 88% chance of a December hike, a 5.07% 10Y and a 7% stronger dollar (against 41% overseas revenue) weigh on the stock. At 9.4x EV/EBIT, though, little rate premium is left to compress.

## Valuation
Bear $223 / base $409 / bull $571 (industry beta: $214 / $391 / $545). The short seller's bear case is $167–184. The price sits about 1% above the analyst's bear case. Downside to the short's case is about 22%, and upside to base is about 81%.
