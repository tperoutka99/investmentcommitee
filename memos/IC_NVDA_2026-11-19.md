# IC Memo: NVDA (NVIDIA Corporation)
**Date:** 2026-11-19 | **Price:** $145.98 | **Market cap:** $3,518B | **Verdict:** WATCH
**Conviction:** Medium | **Position:** None

## Verdict
The business is cheap on its own numbers. But $145.98 sits exactly on the $146 base-case value, which leaves no margin of safety for an unexplained CEO resignation, $118B of verified customer guarantees and an unfiled Q3 10-Q. **This becomes a BUY only when both conditions hold:** the Q3 10-Q is filed with no export-control or Megaspeed disclosure, and the price is at or below $114 (the short seller's own value at a 35% margin).

## Thesis
- At 50% margins the price implies only ~11.9% a year revenue growth for 5 years. Q3 grew 103% YoY and Q4 is guided to $130B ±3%.
- ROIC is 141% (109% with R&D capitalized), and TTM FCF is $148.7B.
- It trades at 14.5x EV/EBIT and 15.7x TTM P/E. Peers trade at 20–98x EV/EBIT.

## Macro backdrop
**Headwind, Medium confidence.** The 10Y is at 5.10% and futures price a December hike at >90%. The short seller has the channel right: higher rates hit customer credit first, and NVIDIA backs up to $118B of it.

## Valuation
At this price the market is pricing either ~11.9% growth at a 50% margin, or consensus growth with margins falling to ~28%.
- **Bear:** $51 (analyst) or ~$68 (short seller), which is 53–65% downside.
- **Base:** $146.
- **Bull:** $300, about 2x.

The price sits at base. The short seller's rebuild of the model gives $153, or $114 at a 35% margin. The net-cash correction in flag 4 lowers every scenario.

## Bear case and response
| Bear point | Status | Response |
|---|---|---|
| NVIDIA is paying for its own demand | Partly resolved | The $118B is verified. But the $105B starts in FY2029 and grows building by building, and the $32B of neocloud deals depends on the neoclouds getting financing. The 8-K itself says customers are outgrowing their balance sheets. |
| Governance and export-control risk | Unresolved | The 8-K gives no reason for the resignation. The probe exists only in web sources. The 10-Q is not filed, and the General Counsel filed a Form 144 on the same day. |
| Earnings quality is peaking | Partly resolved | Q3 net equity gains were only $1.8B against $115.8B of revenue. The $25.5B nine-month figure is unchecked. The gross-margin guide cut to 71–72% is real. |

## Data flags
1. **CEO resignation (Verified):** this raises the governance discount. The "foreign intelligence" statement is in no 8-K, so I treat it as unconfirmed.
2. **Guarantees (Verified):** the credit loop is real, but most of it is years out. It is a 2029+ risk, not a Q4 one.
3. **Receivables (Partly verified):** DSO rose to 64 from 60. The company attributes this to investment-grade customers, and the "up to one year" claim was not found, which eases the concern slightly.
4. **Net cash (Contradicted):** net cash is ~$15.4B, not $59.6B. After the two pending deals it is roughly zero, so the balance-sheet cushion in the bull thesis mostly disappears.
5. **Equity gains (Verified in part):** the Q3 distortion is small. This weakens bear point #3.
6. **Export probe (Not found):** the risk stays open, and the 10-Q is the test.
7. **Hugging Face (Verified pending):** the analyst's $0.66/share deal deduction stands.

## Kill criteria
- Any 10-Q or 8-K says NVIDIA is a subject of the Megaspeed probe, or the board's investigation reports misconduct by Huang → PASS.
- Q4 FY27 revenue comes in below $126.1B (the low end of the guide) or gross margin below 71% → PASS.
- Next quarter's DSO is above 64 days, or maximum guarantee exposure is above $118.0B → PASS.
