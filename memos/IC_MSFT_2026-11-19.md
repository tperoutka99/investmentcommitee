# IC Memo: MSFT (Microsoft Corporation)
**Date:** 2026-11-19 | **Price:** $498.30 | **Market cap:** $3,700B | **Verdict:** WATCH
**Conviction:** Medium | **Position:** None

## Verdict
WATCH. This is an excellent business, but at $498.30 the stock trades about 25% above the analyst's $398 base value. To justify the price, revenue has to compound at about 21% a year for five years. Meanwhile capital efficiency is weakening, with FY26 sales-to-capital at 0.51 against the 0.60 the model assumes. **BUY trigger:** a close at or below $400, near the base value.

## Thesis
- Demand is real. Commercial backlog (RPO) is $677B, up 73%, which is about 2x trailing revenue. Azure grew 48% and is guided to 49–50%.
- Returns on capital are still high. R&D-adjusted return on capital is 24.3% against a 10.5% WACC (the cost of capital).
- Cash generation is the problem. FCF margin fell from 32.9% (FY22) to 20.2% (FY26) as capex went from $24B to $116B, and capex is guided to about $170B for calendar 2026.

## Macro backdrop
**Headwind, Medium confidence.** The 10Y Treasury is at 5.07%, and futures put the odds of a Dec 9 hike above 90%. That pressures a long-duration stock like MSFT. The dollar (UUP) is up 7.0% in six months, and 46.4% of revenue comes from outside the US.

## Valuation
At today's price, the market is pricing in roughly the consensus growth path (25%→16% over five years at a 45% margin), or else a 54.5% margin on base growth.
- Bear: $211 (the short seller's bear case is $262)
- Base: $398
- Bull: $602

The price sits about 25% above base and about 17% below bull. At the short seller's 0.40 sales-to-capital, base value falls to $346. Terminal value is about 73% of the total.

## Data flags
1. **Debt (macro contradicted):** Bonds rise to about $60.5B, with $2.0B floating. Interest cost changes little, but the flag strengthens the bear point that capex is now funded with debt.
2. **OpenAI (partly verified):** The concentration risk is confirmed. I do not use the unverified 39% RPO figure.
3. **Risk factor (verified):** Management itself warns that revenue may not keep pace with capex. This supports bear point #3.
4. **Cash $72.7B (verified):** Net debt of $61.4B and EV of $3,762B stand. No change to my view.
5. **Debt definition:** Not a conflict. If the note proceeds are held as cash, net debt is unchanged, so no valuation change.
6. **Sales-to-capital (judgment, not checked):** I lean toward the actual FY26 figure of 0.51, which makes $398 a ceiling rather than a floor for base value.

## Kill criteria (drop from watch / PASS)
- Q2 FY27 Azure growth comes in below 49% in constant currency, or revenue below the $96B guide.
- Any reported quarter where operating cash flow minus capex is negative.
- The OpenAI receivable rises above $8.5B while OpenAI revenue stays flat at or below $10.0B.
