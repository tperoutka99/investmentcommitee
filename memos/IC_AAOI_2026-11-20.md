# IC Memo: AAOI (Applied Optoelectronics, Inc.)
**Date:** 2026-11-20 | **Price:** $39.46 | **Market cap:** $3,582M | **Verdict:** WATCH
**Conviction:** Low | **Position:** None

## Verdict
The price is below every base value in the reports. But the $58 base leaves out a verified $550M ATM (about 15% dilution) and assumes a 1.5 sales-to-capital ratio that no one has tested. The Q3 profit was also verified as non-operating. **BUY trigger:** Q4 results show all three of revenue ≥ $385M, positive GAAP operating income and a Digicomm receivable below $234.5M, with the price still ≤ $48.6.

## Thesis
- Growth is real: the Q4 guide is $385–420M against Q3's $269.9M, and 9-month revenue is up 90.7% y/y.
- It is cheap on forward sales: EV is 2.0x Q4-annualized sales, against 4.3–12.8x for optical peers.
- The price asks for little margin: the reverse DCF needs 11.6%, against 23% at LITE and 14% at CIEN/VIAV.

## Macro backdrop
**Headwind, Low confidence.** The 10Y is at 5.07% and a Dec 9 hike is priced near 100%. The short seller is right that the channel that matters is the price of ATM stock sales and hyperscaler funding, not the ~$0.5M of interest per hike.

## Valuation
At $39.46 the market implies an 11.6% margin on base growth, or about 68% of base growth at a 15% margin. The analyst's values are bear $9 / base $58 / bull $166, with $48.6 using the Semiconductor beta and $31–65 across the ERP/beta range. The short seller's corrected base (sales-to-capital 0.8, ATM shares added) is $31, with a bear case of $20. The price is 32% below $58 and about 19% below $48.6, in the lower half of $31–65. Terminal value is 89% of base, so that gap is fragile.

## Data flags
- Price check: no flag. $39.46 and $3,582M stand.
- ATM (Verified): lowers per-share value and breaks the "build is funded" pillar. This is the main reason this is not a BUY.
- Digicomm (Verified): the concentration is real. "Collection has been good" softens it, so the Q4 balance is the test.
- Q3 profit (Verified): there are no operating earnings yet. Q4 has to show them.
- Cash $605.4M (Verified): the $380M net cash is real, so solvency is not the near-term risk.
- Beta (method): the stock's own beta is 3.991 against the 0.95 used, so I treat $48.6 as my working base, not $58.
- Sales-to-capital (partly checked): PP&E +$667.9M confirms heavy spending and leans toward the short seller's lower ratio.
- Capacity cut (Not checked): given no weight. The end-2026 target of >500k units a month is checkable.

## Kill criteria
- Q4 revenue below $385M (the low end of guidance): move to PASS.
- The next filing shows the Digicomm receivable above $234.5M, or any reserve against it: PASS.
- Any new ATM or 424B5 filing beyond the $550M program before Q4 results: PASS.
