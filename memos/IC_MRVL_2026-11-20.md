# IC Memo: MRVL (Marvell Technology, Inc.)
**Date:** 2026-11-20 | **Price:** $138.47 (11/19 close; $132.63 pre-market 11/20) | **Market cap:** $121.4B | **Verdict:** WATCH
**Conviction:** Low | **Position:** None

## Verdict
WATCH. Growth is real: Q3 is guided +52% y/y. But the price sits above base value under both betas ($103 / $122), even at $132.63 pre-market. **BUY trigger:** a close at or below $108 (the pro forma base without synergies is $108.5), plus a Dec 1 report showing Q3 revenue of at least $3.087B (the low end of the narrowed ±2% range) and non-GAAP gross margin of at least 57.5%.

## Thesis
- Near-term demand holds up: the Q3 guide is $3.15B (+52% y/y), and preliminary Q3 came in at the midpoint (IR, Nov 2).
- The price needs a lot to go right: a 20.5% revenue CAGR for 10 years at a 33% margin, or a 43.5% margin at base growth (bottom-up beta).
- The balance sheet is not the risk: cash is $3,932.8M (Verified), all $4,999.9M of debt is fixed-rate, and net debt/EBITDA is 0.36x.

## Macro backdrop
**Headwind, Medium confidence.** The 10Y is 5.07%, and betting markets give a Dec 9 hike about 92% odds. That raises the discount rate on a high-multiple chip stock, even though the company's own rate and currency exposure is small.

## Valuation
- **Bottom-up beta:** bear $41, base $103, bull $158. The price is 64% of the way from base to bull.
- **Industry beta:** $48 / $122 / $187.
- **Lumentum pro forma, no synergies:** $66.8 / $108.5 / $145.0.
- **Short seller's bear:** about $70.
- The bull case's 46% imputed ROC is flagged as aggressive.

## Data flags
- **Price check:** a 4.2% lower quote narrows the gap but is still above $122. No change.
- **1. Deal terms:** the $3.2B regulatory fee is real downside.
- **2. Price path:** weakens the "market verdict on the deal" argument.
- **3. Google warrant:** confirms up to $120B of custom-revenue optionality, but adds 58.97M shares of dilution.
- **4. Cash:** supports the clean balance sheet.
- **5. Commitments:** strengthens bear point 2.
- **6. Accrual:** I rely only on the verified six-month rise. The FY25 base was not checked.
- **7. CFO change and 8. board expansion:** both orderly. Governance concern is lower.
- **9. Insider sales:** the CEO's sale was under a 10b5-1 plan and the CAO's was a tax-withholding disposal. This softens the insider red flag.
- **10. TD Cowen:** the downgrade is real, but I don't use the $175 target.
- **11. $314 mean target:** ignored.
- **12. Election cause:** excluded. The cause of the Nov 5 −16.4% drop is unknown.
- **13. Lumentum share count:** excluded from the thesis. The dilution size is unconfirmed.
- **14. Receivables and SBC:** not used. The year-end comparison cuts against the short seller.
- **15. Earnings date:** treated as expected, pending confirmation.
- **16. Beta gap:** the price is above base under both betas, so the choice doesn't flip the verdict.

## Kill criteria
Any of these drops MRVL from WATCH to PASS:
- On Dec 1, Q3 revenue comes in below $3.087B or non-GAAP gross margin below 57.5%.
- The Q3 10-Q shows the variable-consideration accrual above $850M.
- Regulators formally challenge the Lumentum deal, putting the $3.2B fee in play.
