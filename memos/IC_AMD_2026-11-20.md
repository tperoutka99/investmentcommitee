# IC Memo: AMD (Advanced Micro Devices, Inc.)
**Date:** 2026-11-20 | **Price:** $433.73 | **Market cap:** $717.9B | **Verdict:** WATCH

## Verdict
WATCH. The business is growing very fast, but $433.73 is 17% above the analyst's $371 base value. To justify today's price AMD needs a 43.9% target margin, against 17.7% TTM GAAP operating margin. We buy at or below $335, which is about 10% under base value.

## Thesis
- Real growth: Data Center was $8.5B of $13.4B Q3'26 revenue, up 97% y/y. FY2026 revenue of $52,166M is in line with the $52,230M consensus (equity).
- Strong balance sheet: $15,776M strict cash (V1 Verified) against $9,211M of debt, so $6,565M net cash, and all notes are fixed-rate.
- The price is ahead of the value: reverse DCF needs FY27 +78.5% and a 10-year CAGR of 20.7%, or a 43.9% margin. Base is $371 and the short's 30%-margin case is $288.

## Macro backdrop
Headwind, Low confidence. The 10Y is at 5.07% and a hike on 2026-12-09 is priced at about 92%, which pressures a stock on 93x P/E. Strong GDP (3.37%) and capex help demand.

## Valuation
Bottom-up beta 1.84 and WACC 13.06% give bear $143, base $371, bull $595 (industry beta: $154 / $403 / $647). The price sits 17% above base and 27% below bull, so the market is pricing close to the bull case. The short's bear price is $255. Base TV share is 64.8%. Imputed ROC above 40% in base years 4-10 makes even the base look generous.
