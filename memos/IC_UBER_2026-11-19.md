# IC Memo: UBER (Uber Technologies, Inc.)
**Date:** 2026-11-19 | **Price:** $65.60 | **Market cap:** $133.95B | **Verdict:** WATCH
**Conviction:** Medium | **Position:** None

## Verdict
The price already discounts a lot. At $65.60 it implies about 8.4% revenue growth at a flat 12.4% margin, close to Q3's 9.6%, and insiders have been buying above today's price. The deciding issue is the balance sheet. A reported $14.8B Delivery Hero deal on a ~€14B bridge loan has not been checked against any filing, and it would take net debt to about 2.5x EBITDA just as the Fed starts hiking. **Trigger to BUY (starter):** (a) a filing confirms Delivery Hero pro forma net debt/EBITDA of 2.5x or less, including Delivery Hero's own debt, **and** Q4'26 revenue growth is 9.6% YoY or higher; **or** (b) the price closes at or below $50, the short seller's bear value.

## Thesis
- The price implies about 8.4% growth, against 20% Q3 bookings growth and a ~13% consensus revenue CAGR.
- FCF yield is 7.8% (6.2% after stock comp). Even the short seller's owner FCF is $6.86B.
- Insiders are buying: the CEO bought $10.0M at $70.96 and the COO bought $5.3M, for net buying of +$10.8M.

## Macro backdrop
**Headwind, Medium confidence.** The 10Y is at 5.10%, CPI is about 3.7%, and futures give a >90% chance of a hike on Dec 9, which squeezes the multiple. With debt now at $17.74B, I accept the short seller's point that rates also hit Uber through interest cost.

## Valuation
The price implies either 8.4% growth at a 12.4% margin, or 13% growth with the margin falling to 10.2%. Bear: $47 (DCF) and $50 (owner FCF at 15x). Base: $116, or about $95 once WACC is raised by roughly 1pt for the 5.10% 10Y. Bull: $185. The price sits just above the bear values and well below the rate-adjusted base. The upside is bigger than the downside, but the base case needs 13% growth, which Q3's 9.6% does not yet support.

## Bear case and response
| Bear point | Status | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue and margins slowing | Unresolved | Q3 revenue grew 9.6%, op margin fell to 9.8%, and take rate dropped from 27.1% to 24.7%. Q4 driver incentives will cut take rate further. The UK change explains only part of it. |

| Debt rising; buybacks only offset dilution | Unresolved | Debt rose from $12.08B to $17.74B in nine months. Delivery Hero would take net debt to ~$19B. Basic shares are +0.1% vs FY23 despite $12.05B of buybacks. |

## Data flags
1. **Risk-free rate:** I use 5.10%, so the base falls to about $95. Upside shrinks but is still larger than downside.
2. **Net debt ($7.67B vs $8.18B):** Doesn't change the verdict. I use the higher $8.18B.
3. **M&A conflict:** This is the deciding flag. I won't open a position until a filing confirms the Delivery Hero terms.
4. **Equity stakes:** I take the ~$4.3/share haircut and rely on EV/EBIT ex-stakes (17.2x).
5. **Share count:** The two figures don't contradict each other. I give buybacks no credit beyond offsetting dilution.

## Kill criteria
- Q4'26 revenue growth comes in below 8.4% YoY, the rate the price implies. Move to PASS.
- A filing shows Delivery Hero pro forma net debt/EBITDA above 2.5x. Move to PASS.
- Q4'26 non-GAAP EPS comes in below $0.95 or adj. EBITDA below $3.13B (the low ends of guidance). Move to PASS.
