# IC Memo: UBER (Uber Technologies, Inc.)
**Date:** 2026-11-19 | **Price:** $65.60 | **Market cap:** $133.95B | **Verdict:** WATCH
**Conviction:** Medium | **Position:** None

## Verdict
The stock is cheap on headline numbers: 15.0x 2027 EPS, and the price implies margins below today's. But the base case of $104 needs an 18% margin. Owner FCF yields only 4.9%, below the 5.10% 10Y. We also still can't size Delivery Hero leverage on floating-rate debt (Verified). **BUY (starter) trigger:** the price closes at or below $52, **or** an Uber filing shows pro forma net debt/EBITDA of 2.5x or less including Delivery Hero **and** Q4'26 non-GAAP EPS comes in at $0.95 or higher.

## Thesis
- The price implies an 11.5% long-run GAAP margin at 13% growth, against 12.4% TTM today.
- Gross bookings have grown 20%+ for five straight quarters, and trips are up 16% to 4.1B.
- Insiders are buying above the price: the CEO bought $10.0M at $70.96.

## Macro backdrop
**Headwind, Medium.** The 10Y is at 5.10%, CPI is about 4%, and the market prices about an 85% chance of a December hike. The term loans float (Verified), so higher rates now hit interest cost as well as the multiple.

## Valuation
At $65.60 the price implies an 11.5% margin at 13% growth, or 3.8% growth at an 18% margin. The DCF gives bear $52, base $104 and bull $175, so the price sits near the bottom of the range. The short seller's bear value is −21% from here. Haircuts pull the realistic value down. With no moat, the base is $83–88. At a 14% margin, the model gives $52–83. Owner FCF at consensus growth is worth about $63.5, roughly today's price.

## Data flags
1. **Floating debt (Verified for the short seller):** The macro report understated the rate risk. I treat the rate headwind as hitting both valuation and earnings. The $23.7B pro forma net debt is unverified.
2. **Delivery Hero:** The deal terms and the €700M fee are Verified, but the 66.56% acceptance is Not found. I treat the deal as likely, costly to abandon, and unsized because Delivery Hero's own debt is unknown. This is the gating flag.
3. **Cash-flow quality (inputs Verified):** I use a range of 4.9–6.2% rather than 7.8%. That removes the "cheap on FCF" argument.
4. **Waymo (Not found, not contradicted):** I don't treat the Jan 2028 date as fact, but I keep the no-moat haircut because direct competition is in the filings.
5. **Dilution (different bases):** No effect. I credit buybacks only for net share reduction.

## Kill criteria
- An Uber filing shows pro forma net debt/EBITDA above 3.0x including Delivery Hero: move to PASS.
- Q4'26 gross bookings come in below $63.25B or non-GAAP EPS below $0.95 (the low ends of guidance): move to PASS.
- Q4'26 Mobility revenue declines year on year for a second straight quarter: move to PASS.
