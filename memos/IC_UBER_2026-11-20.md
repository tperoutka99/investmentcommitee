# IC Memo: UBER (Uber Technologies, Inc.)
**Date:** 2026-11-20 | **Price:** $65.60 | **Market cap:** $133.96B | **Verdict:** WATCH
**Conviction:** Medium | **Position:** None

## Verdict
On the analyst's model Uber looks cheap ($121 base). After haircuts it looks fairly priced. Its like-for-like margin is 11.6%, which is the margin $65.60 already implies, and a more realistic beta pulls the base value down to $62. **BUY (starter) trigger:** a close at or below $53, the short seller's bear value. At that price, owner cash flow of $6.52B yields 6%, about 1 point above the 10Y.

## Thesis
- TTM FCF is $10.4B (7.8% yield). Uber trades at 19.9x EBIT; DoorDash trades at 71.9x.
- Operating margin rose from 3.0% (2023) to 12.4% TTM. Mobility operating income grew 27% in Q3.
- Insiders bought about $15.3M in 6 months and sold about $4.5M.

## Macro backdrop
**Neutral, Medium.** Strong US demand (GDP 3.4%, payrolls +165k) supports the 52.9% of revenue from the US and Canada. A 3.0% dollar rise and a 5.07% 10Y offset that. All drawn debt is fixed-rate, so rates hit the valuation multiple, not interest cost.

## Valuation
The price implies about 1.1% growth at a 20% margin, or an 11.6% margin at base growth. The DCF gives bear $59 / base $121 / bull $192, so the price sits 11% above the bear. Haircuts: beta 1.3 gives $62. A 12–13% margin gives $68–75. The short seller's bear is $53. That puts the realistic range at $53–75, and the price is in the middle of it, so there is no margin of safety.

## Data flags
1. Margin basis: I use 11.6%. Q3's 9.8% leaves the 20% base margin unproven.
2. UK VAT: $1.5B more cash out; lowers FCF.
3. Waymo: I treat the 2028 split as likely. Cars stay on Uber until May 2028. The Phoenix month correction (May, not June) doesn't change the view.
4. Delivery Hero terms: the €700M fee makes the deal costly to abandon.
5. Floating debt: zero drawn, which removes the prior interest-cost headwind.
6. Cash $10.07B: net debt of $5.72B stands.
7. Q4 guidance: used for kill thresholds.
8. Insurance reserves: the 4.9% yield is my floor case, not a fact.
9. Equity stakes: about a $0.30/share gap; immaterial.
10. Beta: I weight the $62 value heavily.
11. Insider buying (not checked): supportive, not relied on.
