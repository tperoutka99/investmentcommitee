## Short seller
# Short Seller: SPCX, 2026-11-20

**1. Top 3 bear points**
1. **Most of the SI growth comes from one rival AI lab that can leave on 90 days' notice.** "Customer B" was 34.5% of Q3 revenue (about $5.0B of SI's $7.56B), and it was under 10% in 2025. The Q2 10-Q added a new risk factor: cloud agreements "may be terminated by either party upon 90 days' notice" once the ramp ends. (EDGAR 10-Q 2026-08-04; 10-Q 2026-10-22 Note 2) Bloomberg says Customer B is Anthropic, and that Anthropic's commitment can be canceled after early 2027 (web, unconfirmed). Take that revenue out and Q3 consolidated operating income of $2.79B turns into a loss.
2. **The business burns cash, and Q3 profit is low quality.** Over 9M26, free cash flow was −$35.0B (OCF $13.1B less capex $48.1B), against net income of −$0.4B. A further $11.8B of capex sits unpaid in payables. Commitments for 2027 total $25.6B, and the Apollo GPU lease adds about $40B more. (EDGAR 10-Q; 8-K 2026-10-26) Cash fell from $100.8B on June 19 to $89.9B at September 30. (EDGAR 8-K 2026-06-22) Q3 net income of $4.4B includes $1.03B of other income, of which $561M is a mark-up on Musk-controlled TBC, plus a $449M valuation-allowance release. Adjusted EBITDA leaves out SBC of $2.36B, which was 16.2% of revenue.
3. **Heavy dilution, share supply and conflicts.** Shares went from 9,649M pro forma for FY25 to 13,579M (+41%). Still to come: 262M EchoStar shares, a 302M-share CEO award, a 301M plan pool, and 73M Cursor RSUs and options. Lock-up releases: up to 798M shares on Dec 8, then 352M after Q4 results. (EDGAR 424B4) The board is weighing a Tesla combination, with the controlling shareholder on both sides. (EDGAR 10-Q MD&A)

**2. Weakest assumption:** the base case's sales-to-capital of 0.8. Revenue has risen $13.4B since FY25 (TTM), while net PP&E rose $46.9B, which is about 0.29 before counting the $36.6B of new goodwill. A ratio of 0.4–0.5 is more realistic. Note that even at 0.8 the analyst's base ($47.45) and bull ($103.28) values are both below the $131.97 price.
