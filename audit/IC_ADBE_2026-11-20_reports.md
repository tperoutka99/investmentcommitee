## Short seller
# ADBE: Short Seller Report (2026-11-20)

**1. Top 3 bear points**
1. **Organic growth is slowing fast, and Q4 is a stretch.** Total ARR growth went from 11.5% (FY25) to 12.5% (Q2) to 11.2% (Q3), and that includes about $480M from Semrush. Excluding Semrush, growth falls from 10.5% to about 9.2%. Net new ARR was about $0.40B in Q3, against about $0.65B a year earlier (down 38%). The FY26 guide (+10.2% on $25.66B) needs about $0.78B in Q4, roughly double Q3, which implies about 8.3% organic exit growth (10-K 2026-01-15; 10-Qs 2026-06-15 and 2026-09-22, EDGAR). Calculated billings rose 8.4% in Q3 against 12.9% revenue growth, because deferred revenue fell quarter on quarter (XBRL, EDGAR). Wells Fargo made the same Q4 point when it downgraded (web).
2. **Management is leaving as AI competition arrives.** The CFO resigned in June and an interim CFO replaced him (8-K 2026-06-11). The CEO retires on Dec 1, the head of Creativity & Productivity left on Sept 27 (8-K 2026-09-08), and retention severance was set up for an executive (8-K 2026-07-17). The new CEO comes from the slower marketing business. In Q1 FY26 the segments were merged, so Creative is no longer reported separately (10-Q, EDGAR). There was also a $70M goodwill impairment in Q2.
3. **Profits are growing much more slowly than revenue.** Q3 GAAP operating income rose 8.3% and net income 3.1% on 12.9% revenue growth. The tax rate rose to 22% from 19% (10-Q 2026-09-22). FY25's 28% net-income jump flattered the trailing P/E of 11.8x. About $18.1B of buybacks since FY25 bought around 50M shares at about $362 each, against $221.63 today.

**2. Weakest assumption:** base growth of **9.5% in FY27**, fading to 4%. Organic ARR is exiting near 8.3% and decelerating, and Semrush stops adding to growth from Q3 FY27. FY27 should be about **6.5–7%**, fading to 3%. Applied to a GAAP-margin path in the low-to-mid 30s (not 37%), this cuts most of the gap between $409 and the current price.

**4. Macro:** "Headwind" is right, but for the wrong reason. At 9.4x EV/EBIT there is little long-duration premium left for higher rates to squeeze. Refinancing $2.15B adds only about $30–60M. The risk is specific to Adobe (AI substitution), and seat demand has decoupled from solid US GDP, so the "modest positive" from growth is overstated.
