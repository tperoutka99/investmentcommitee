**3. Sensitivity**
- **Dollar (biggest exposure):** 46.4% of Sep-quarter revenue ($42.8B of $92.3B) came from outside the US, and 48.5% for FY26 ($161.0B). A dollar that is about 7% stronger cuts the dollar value of that foreign revenue. Before any hedging, that could take up to about 3 points off reported revenue. UUP tracks a euro-heavy basket that may not match MSFT's currency mix, and its hedging is not assessed (no data).
- **Rates:** The company's own interest cost barely moves. All $35.5B of debt is fixed-rate notes at 2.63–5.30%, and only $4.4B matures by Jun 2027. Refinancing that $4.4B at today's roughly 5% yields costs tens of millions of dollars a year. The floating-rate share created by swaps is not assessed (no data). The real rate risk is valuation: a long-duration software/cloud stock with the 10Y above 5% and more hikes priced in.
- **Growth:** This is a tailwind. 53.6% of revenue is US, where GDP grew 3.4% and the Fed describes capital investment as "robust," which supports enterprise IT and cloud spending.
- **Inflation:** Recurring software revenue gives some ability to raise prices. The main harm is indirect, through the hiking path and the dollar.

**4. Call:** **Headwind, Medium confidence.** Strong demand is being outweighed by a 7% dollar rally hitting almost half of revenue and a Fed that is still hiking with the 10Y above 5%.

## 3. Short seller (red team)

**MSFT short case (as of 2026-11-19)**

**1. Top 3 bear points**
1. **Much of the backlog depends on two AI labs that burn cash.** OpenAI paid Microsoft $10.0B last quarter, 10.8% of revenue. What OpenAI owes Microsoft rose from $6.0B to $7.2B in one quarter. Microsoft owns about 24% of OpenAI and has funded $12.0B of its $13.0B commitment (EDGAR 10-Q, 2026-10-28). The FY26 10-K said no customer was over 10% of revenue; OpenAI is now over that line (EDGAR 10-K, 2026-07-29). OpenAI is roughly 32% of RPO, derived from the CFO saying RPO grew 25% excluding OpenAI (web, Benzinga). Anthropic's Azure commitment is $47.2B, about 7% (web, Trefis). Together that is about 39% of the $677B. New 10-Q wording: partners "are significant customers of Azure" and "expected consumption… may not materialize" (EDGAR).
2. **Cash flow is disappearing.** Free cash flow fell from 0.84x net income (FY24) to 0.50x (FY26) (FMP). Q1 FY27: $58.0B operating cash minus $39.4B capex minus $12.9B of component prepayments leaves $5.7B, 0.16x net income. Add $9.9B of new finance leases and the quarter is cash-negative. Another $364.6B of leases has not started yet (EDGAR 10-Q). Cash fell $12.3B in the quarter, then Microsoft sold $25B of new notes at 4.85–6.35% (EDGAR 8-K, 2026-11-04).
3. **Margins are at a peak.** Trailing depreciation ($42.1B) is only 0.31x capex ($135.9B), so it has to rise a lot. Microsoft Cloud gross margin is already down to 66% (EDGAR 10-Q). A new risk factor says the spending comes "in advance of fully developed revenue streams" and could lead to "impairment" (EDGAR). Anthropic launched an Excel rival on Nov 10 and MSFT fell 2.2% (web).

**2. Weakest assumption:** sales-to-capital of 0.60 in the base case (0.80 bull). FY26 actual: $50.1B of new revenue on $97.3B net reinvestment = 0.51, before counting finance leases. Q1's run-rate including leases is about 0.3. I rebuilt the analyst's model (within 3% of all three of his cases). At 0.40, base value is $346, and even the 25%→16% growth path today's price assumes gives only $419.

**3. Red flag checklist**
- Insider selling: **Watch.** About 185k shares (~$92M) sold since May 19 by Nadella, Hood, Althoff and Numoto, with no open-market buys. The CEO and CFO sold under 10b5-1 plans (EDGAR Form 4).
- Institutions: **Watch.** Pershing cut its stake 34.5% in Q3; Coatue doubled (web).
- Dilution: **Clear.** Diluted shares down 0.35% over 3 years; stock comp is 3.4% of revenue.
- Receivables: **Clear** (+17.4% vs revenue +18.8%). OpenAI's receivable up 20% in one quarter: **Watch.**
- Inventory: **Watch.** Up 62% vs revenue up 19%, but only $1.8B.
- Free cash flow vs net income: **Red.**
- Guidance cuts: **Watch.** No cut, but operating margin is guided "down slightly."
- Downgrades and target cuts: **Clear.** None in 6 months, one upgrade; consensus target $617 (FMP).
- News trend: **Watch.** Stock went from $545 to $498 since Nov 9 (web).
- New risk factors: **Red.** New sections on capex running ahead of revenue, impairment, access to capital and credit ratings, power supply, and local opposition to datacenters (Sep vs Mar 10-Q).
- One-time items: FY26 net income includes about $5.0B of OpenAI gains (EDGAR 10-K).

**4. Where the macro call is wrong:** "$35.5B, all fixed-rate" is out of date. The Nov 4 8-K adds $25B, including $2B floating, for about $1.4B a year more interest. The bigger miss is that rates reach Microsoft through its customers: about 39% of the backlog depends on AI labs that need capital markets to pay their bills. The 10-Q itself now lists "interest rates, credit markets… credit ratings" as a risk to the capex plan. Funding drives this demand, not GDP growth.

**5. Bear case price: about $260 (−48%).** Same model with growth of 17% falling to 8% (backlog cut toward the ex-OpenAI RPO growth of 25%), a 42% margin as depreciation catches up, sales-to-capital of 0.40 and a terminal return on capital of 15%. Result: $262.

**6. Thesis strength: 3/5.** The business is excellent and demand is real. But at $498 the stock needs consensus growth and better capital efficiency than Microsoft is showing.
